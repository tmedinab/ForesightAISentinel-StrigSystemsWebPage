# Strig Systems / Athene — independent identity review

Prepared for a professional brand designer. Version: 2026-10-07. Client: Tomás Medina, Strig Systems, Concepción, Chile. Two candidate identity systems, A and B. These are development studies, not approved production masters.

## 1. Assignment

Evaluate each company/product pair on its own merits, then compare the two systems. We need an independent diagnosis of concept, character, formal execution, distinctiveness and practical use. Identify what deserves to survive, what creates ambiguity, and the highest-value refinement. You may recommend retaining, evolving or replacing a direction; please explain the benefit and the identity it would sacrifice. A synthesis is optional, not an obligation to combine pieces from both proposals.

Suggested review sequence: inspect `boards/03_AB_comparison.svg` and record first impressions before reading the route explanations below. Then inspect the individual boards, reduction sheet and vector files. Finally read the construction notes and client preferences. This separates initial perception from supplied narrative; it is not a blind audience study.

## 2. Business and brand architecture

**Strig Systems** is the parent engineering and development company. It must accommodate future products and technologies. The company identity should communicate sound engineering judgment, precision and the ability to bring parts into a coherent system. It should not lock the business to a particular aircraft, two sensors, forestry alone or the current first product.

**Athene** is the first system: an experimental aerial sentinel for forestry and wildfire-related territorial risk monitoring, with a focus on the night-time visibility gap. Its intended architecture combines aerial observation, onboard inference and human oversight of decisions. Protection and earlier awareness are objectives, not guaranteed operational outcomes. The website describes an experimental TRL 3 development stage; the identity must not imply a mature deployed fleet or demonstrated performance that has not been established.

In corporate headers and general documents, Strig leads. On a system sheet, Athene leads and Strig appears as the developer. Do not collapse the two brands into one emblem. Noctua and Nest are associated platform names; separate emblems for them are outside this assignment.

## 3. Audience and intended character

Primary audiences are forestry stakeholders, technical decision makers and institutions evaluating early validation partnerships. Engineering peers and investment audiences are secondary. The desired character is a commanding visual presence: composed, competent, professional, elegant and reassuring rather than intimidating.

The client named Lockheed Martin Skunk Works, Anduril Industries, Quantum Systems, Skydio Defense, Helsing AI and General Atomics as aesthetic reference territories: aerospace engineering, avionics, disciplined geometry and functional contrast. These are client references, not proof that we follow their internal design processes. Do not copy their marks, trade dress or distinctive devices. Do not equate a defense aesthetic with hostility or with the maturity of those companies.

The owl naming root references Strigiformes and Athene. It supplies identity and a sentinel metaphor. For Strig, the agreed priority is abstract engineering/perception first, with an owl as a possible secondary reading. Athene may be more recognizably owl-like. An implicit S, SS or A is an optional resource, not a requirement to force lettering into the anatomy. No claim about thermal or infrared vision in real owls is intended.

## 4. Option A — open field and frontal sentinel

**Company:** H1, an open, faceted corporate symbol composed of two opposing shapes. Its organization has 180-degree rotational symmetry about the center of the 128-unit canvas. The open middle and controlled diagonals suggest a continuous abstract S without requiring an owl illustration.

**Product:** a broad, frontal, open face with prolonged cheek returns. It is the Athene geometry preferred in the R11 study, using the balanced R12 lockup. The center and side returns stay connected; the shape can support an owl reading, but visor, helmet or mask readings remain possible.

**Client feedback:** A is the direction selected earlier for refinement. The client preferred its open cheeks, H1 continuity and the longer-return Athene, which felt more serious. The balanced product composition was explicitly preferred. The corporate optical composition remains a working recommendation. These preferences do not establish external recognition or make A the required winner of this review.

**Questions:** Does the corporate abstraction have enough distinctiveness? Does the product read as attentive and protective, or as a helmet/mask? Is the broad product symbol appropriately imposing beside the name? Does the company/product relationship remain credible despite different symmetry and width?

## 5. Option B — dual path and directional sentinel

**Company:** B3, the longitudinal S with supported turns from R18, preserved in R19. Two continuous filled paths follow the S trajectory. A nominal 4-unit channel separates them. The two central turn locations were moved by 2 units horizontally and 1 vertically relative to the R17 trial, redistributing ink toward the outer turn supports. This is a local construction refinement; its perceptual benefit remains to be assessed. It does not create more separation or prove better small-size recognition.

**Product:** the R15 owl-like profile, preserved without an additional cut. Its posterior opening separates the head/neck region from the lower return. That negative space was not originally designed as a second owl, a wing or a territory map. The profile provides direction and a more figurative, internally solid presence than the dual-path company symbol.

**Client feedback:** the client initially needed time to recognize the owl profile and later appreciated it. He likes the longitudinal S and specifically appreciates B3's reinforced turns. He agrees that repeating the corporate cut in Athene is unnecessary. This makes B3 a liked candidate within B, not a final global selection over A.

**Questions:** Is the corporate symbol perceived as a deliberate S, dual S paths, an outline or an overly intricate zigzag? Does its lighter ink distribution work with the heavier product profile? Does the profile feel like competent attention, or read as sporty/aggressive/otherwise unrelated? Do the name, proportion, direction and return create enough family coherence without matching cuts?

## 6. Typography, geometry and supplied files

The current wordmarks use Space Grotesk: STRIG and ATHENE at weight 700; SYSTEMS at weight 400. The bundled font and SIL Open Font License are included. Current wordmarks are outlined and not custom proprietary lettering. Typography refinement may be recommended; please distinguish it from symbol refinement.

Symbol canvases are 128 × 128 units, with real transparent negative space and `evenodd` fills. Units describe graphic construction, not hardware measurements. Horizontal lockup canvases are 490 × 140. The supplied individual lockups preserve existing geometry and transforms; metadata and fixed ink/white fills are adapted for this handoff. No emblem has been redrawn for export.

At a nominal 85-unit wordmark size, the working corporate composition uses visible symbol height 65.4348, gap 22 and SYSTEMS size 24. The Athene composition uses visible symbol height 59 and gap 24. A's wider face consequently occupies approximately 118 units of width; B's profile approximately 64.9. Optical centering differs by role. These are disclosed working parameters, not final spacing rules.

The comparison and individual boards use the same visible symbol height per role and the same nominal wordmark size. This does not equalize ink area or symbol width. The reduction sheet instead uses a fixed 128-unit canvas and origin, rendered at 16, 24, 32 and 48 px in positive and reversed form. Judge the actual target size; do not infer legibility from enlarged screenshots. A's wide frontal face naturally occupies a different visible area.

SYSTEMS is a practical constraint: small lockups can make it unreadable. Recent context studies enlarged the developer signature rather than changing the internal ratio. Minimum sizes, clear space and micro variants remain to be resolved for actual applications. White assets are transparent and need a dark background.

## 7. Evaluation priorities and requested response

For each route, please distinguish observations from interpretations. Discuss:

1. Initial visual reading and strongest unintended association, before using the concept story.
2. Fit with broad engineering authority for Strig and competent sentinel attention for Athene.
3. Character: presence, elegance, composure and severity; identify the specific features responsible.
4. Formal craft: proportion, mass, negative space, joins, curves/facets, terminals, optical alignment and typography.
5. Family coherence and company/product hierarchy in a header, system sheet and document.
6. Reproduction at small sizes, in monochrome and reversed; separate construction issues from usage-size issues.
7. Distinctiveness within the reference territory and any close resemblance worth investigating. No trademark clearance has been established.
8. What to preserve, the most valuable single intervention, its expected benefit and what it could damage.

After those independent diagnoses, recommend a next direction or a justified hybrid, with a small number of concrete next steps. If useful, include a rough annotated SVG sketch rather than a wholesale polished redesign before scope is agreed. The included response template is optional; do not force numerical aesthetic scores.

## 8. Constraints, provenance and scope

Keep company and product roles distinct. Avoid cartoon birds, decorative feathers, generic shields, tactical decoration and gradients used to rescue weak geometry. Evaluate monochrome structure first. Shared grammar need not mean identical silhouettes or cuts. The client welcomes professional disagreement and evidence-based changes to working design choices.

The work so far is AI-assisted vector design and simulated specialist critique, with direct client feedback. It has not been independently validated with customers or human designers. Internal rasterization, bounds and continuity checks support reproduction; they do not validate meaning, trust, market acceptance or exclusivity. Your review is intended to bring an independent professional perspective to these exported A/B candidates.

Technical photos/renders of the project have unverified origin and are omitted. No CAD, aircraft performance claims, product deployment evidence or operational guarantees should be inferred from this package. Product descriptions explain design intent and stage only.

This handoff does not authorize publication, trademark filing or final adoption. It requests evaluation. Scope for commissioned redesign, production masters, a usage manual or further audience research can be agreed after the diagnosis. Please preserve route IDs in annotated feedback so revisions remain traceable.
